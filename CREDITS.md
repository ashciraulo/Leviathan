## Art and audio

All pixel art is original, generated by `tools/build_art.py` for this
project. Sound effects are placeholder chiptune tones synthesised in code
(`scripts/autoload/audio.gd`); music is still to come. If third-party assets are ever added, list each one
here with its author, source link and license.
